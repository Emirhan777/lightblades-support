Privacy policy

This policy describes how LightBlades for iPhone handles information when you play or contact us.

Camera and motion

With your permission, LightBlades uses the rear camera and ARKit to estimate the phone’s position and uses motion sensors to determine its orientation and movement. The camera is also used to scan a laptop pairing code when you choose that option. Camera images and AR video frames are processed on your iPhone; the app does not save them or send them to the laptop, nearby phone, or developer.

On-device play

When you play on one iPhone, pose information is sent directly to the game running within the app on that phone. No account, developer server, internet connection, or advertising or analytics service is needed for this mode. The music and game assets are bundled in the app.

Connecting devices

If you choose laptop play, the app sends motion and position estimates, timing and tracking status, a session identifier, and the configured phone name and dimensions to the laptop companion over the address you choose. The standard local WebSocket connection is not encrypted, so use a trusted local network. If you join an online laptop room, the app sends the same information over an encrypted connection through the tunnel service chosen by the laptop host; that provider can process connection information under its own privacy policy. The free temporary room option uses Cloudflare. If you choose a nearby second sword, the phones exchange connection and motion information using Apple’s encrypted MultipeerConnectivity session. Nearby discovery may display your device name to another nearby device.

Screen mirroring or screen recording is controlled by iOS and the sharing method you choose. Your chosen destination or recording can show the app’s screen; LightBlades does not control how that destination handles its copy.

Settings stored on your phone

LightBlades saves settings such as the laptop address, phone profile name, and phone dimensions in the app’s local preferences. You can change them in the app; deleting the app removes its locally stored preferences. The laptop companion may store its own interface preferences on the laptop.

Support and third-party services

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address and message, so that we can respond. GitHub issues are public: do not include private information. These support pages are hosted by GitHub, which may process standard web request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. Apple and other services you choose, such as AirPlay, may process information under their own policies.

Your choices

You can manage Camera, Motion & Fitness, Local Network, Bluetooth, and screen-recording access in iOS Settings or Control Center, as applicable. Some features will not work without the relevant permission. You can delete the app to remove its local settings.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact pendrawapp@gmail.com. We may update this policy as the app changes and will revise the date above.
